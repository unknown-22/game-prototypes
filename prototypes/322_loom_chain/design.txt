# LOOM CHAIN — Pyxel Game Prototype Design Spec

## Theme & Fantasy
You are a weaver operating a hand loom. Warp threads (vertical) have FIXED colors.
A shuttle carries the weft thread whose color AUTO-CYCLES. Shoot the shuttle to weave
a cell: if the weft color matches that column's warp color, it is a PERFECT weave
(COMBO chain). Mismatches leave a permanent gray FLAW (a visible log of mistakes).
Completed rows scroll off into a fabric archive — the woven cloth IS your log
("log/replay as asset"). This is the FIRST weaving/loom genre in the collection.

## The One "Fun Moment" (must deliver this)
"Move the shuttle to the column whose warp color matches the current weft color and
shoot just before the color cycles — chaining perfect weaves into a rainbow SUPER
SHUTTLE and banking a PERFECT ROW whose bonus snowballs with consecutive perfect rows."

## Reinterpretation source
game_idea_factory idea #1 (Score 32.55, logistics/delivery deckbuilder):
- "log/replay as asset (previous action becomes next card)" -> the woven fabric is a
  permanent visual log; consecutive PERFECT ROWS amplify future row bonuses (snowball).
- "CA grid fills up (infection/growth) to control" -> the fabric accumulates row by row
  into the archive; a fully-flawed row TANGLES the loom (extra HEAT).

## Distinct mechanic (what makes this NOT another grid-click game)
A SINGLE ACTIVE ROW of 6 cells + a fixed per-column warp color + a spatially-moving
shuttle cursor + a row-completion bonus that scales with CONSECUTIVE PERFECT ROWS.
The player must (a) move the cursor to the right column, (b) time the shot to the
auto-cycling weft color, and (c) keep rows PERFECT to protect the snowball bonus.

## Hard Constraints (Pyxel 2.x)
- Single file: prototypes/322_loom_chain/main.py. Screen 320x240, @60fps (FPS=60).
- English-only text (Pyxel BDF font has NO CJK glyphs). NO emoji.
- Colors (raw ints): BLACK=0 NAVY=1 PURPLE=2 GREEN=3 BROWN=4 DARK_BLUE=5 LIGHT_BLUE=6
  WHITE=7 RED=8 ORANGE=9 YELLOW=10 LIME=11 CYAN=12 GRAY=13 PINK=14 PEACH=15.
- Weave colors: RED=8, LIME=11, DARK_BLUE=5, YELLOW=10. FLAW color = GRAY=13.
- API: pyxel.tri (NOT triangle), pyxel.KEY_RETURN (NOT KEY_ENTER),
  pyxel.MOUSE_BUTTON_LEFT, pyxel.mouse_wheel (NOT KEY_WHEEL_UP/DOWN).
- Just `import pyxel` at top (NO TYPE_CHECKING guard).

## Screens / draw
- TITLE: title "LOOM CHAIN" + controls text + "PRESS ENTER" (use pyxel.KEY_RETURN).
  Also show best_score.
- PLAYING: draw warp threads (vertical lines per column, colored), the active row cells
  (colored squares or GRAY flaws; empty cells = dark outline), the shuttle cursor
  (highlight outline on cursor_col, and a small shuttle block), weft color indicator
  (a colored square + label "WEFT"), HEAT bar (right side, red at high heat),
  TIMER bar (top), SCORE + COMBO text (top-left), SUPER = rainbow border + flashing.
  Draw fabric_log archive as thin horizontal strips scrolling below the board
  (each completed row = a 6-cell strip; perfect rows draw a gold/yellow tint).
- GAME_OVER: "GAME OVER" + final score + best score + "PRESS ENTER TO RETRY".

## Risk / Reward (must be clearly felt)
- Matching = COMBO + score scales with combo; COMBO>=4 = SUPER SHUTTLE (300f rainbow,
  any-color match, 3x score, HEAT decay frozen, weft frozen).
- Mismatch = permanent GRAY flaw + HEAT+15 + combo reset. A flaw in a row breaks the
  consecutive_perfect streak (forfeits the snowball). An all-flaw row = TANGLED (+15 HEAT).
- Keeping rows PERFECT builds the snowball (each perfect row banks 100*streak + 120),
  so "risk a shot for the snowball vs wait for the safe color" is the tension.

## Headless-testing requirements (CRITICAL for test_imports.py)
- import pyxel normally. Tests use Game.__new__(Game) to bypass __init__ (avoiding
  pyxel.init/pyxel.run). Then g = Game.__new__(Game); g.reset(); g.rng = random.Random(42).
- reset() must be self-contained and NOT touch any pyxel.* function.
- Tests call _weave / _complete_row / _advance_weft / _update_heat / cycle_interval
  directly with controlled state (set g.weft_color, g.warp_colors, g.cells manually).
- NEVER call _update_playing or any method touching pyxel.btn/btnp/mouse in tests.
